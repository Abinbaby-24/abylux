ABYLUX Portfolio Demo

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser.

Included:
- Responsive light purple-and-white portfolio
- Mountain/galaxy hero background
- Sticky navigation inspired by the poster
- Services, about, project samples, and contact sections

Before publishing:
- Replace the sample project cards with your real projects.
- Update hello@abylux.com to your actual email.
- Add your WhatsApp, Instagram, GitHub, and domain links.
- The email link is only a placeholder and does not send a form submission.

Navigation logo: your uploaded ABYLUX logo is saved at assets/abylux-logo.png and used in the navbar.
