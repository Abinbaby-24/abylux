FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before getting started.

How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start at ₹3,999, depending on the features and requirements.

How long does it take to build a website?

A basic website may take 3–5 days, while more complex projects may take longer.

Do I need to pay monthly?

Website development is a one-time project fee. Domain, hosting, paid services, and optional maintenance may involve additional charges.

Can customers book appointments through my website?

Yes. Depending on your requirements, we can integrate WhatsApp appointment requests or a more advanced booking system.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. Updates can be arranged separately or through an optional maintenance plan.
